## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Heart disease is the world's leading cause of death, and most people at risk don't know it. Heart Risk Check helps you, your parents and your community find heart risk early and act on it. It's free, it needs no blood test, and your data never leaves your phone.

❤️ TWO-MINUTE HEART CHECK
Answer 13 simple questions and see your risk as "X in 100 people like you". The app shows what raises your risk and which changes would lower it most. The model was trained on almost 130,000 adults.

🩺 BLOOD PRESSURE LOG
Log your readings in seconds. Each one is rated Normal, Elevated, Stage 1, Stage 2 or Very high, with 7- and 30-day averages, a 30-day chart, and an emergency warning for readings above 180/120.

💊 MEDICINE REMINDERS
A daily checklist of your doses, with due doses highlighted, a 7-day adherence score and a streak.

🚨 SOS AND EMERGENCY HELP
• One tap calls your country's ambulance number (46 countries)
• Send your GPS location to family by WhatsApp or SMS
• Heart-attack first aid and CPR steps
• "How urgent is it?" symptom check: call now, today or this week
• Medical ID for paramedics: blood group, allergies, conditions and medicines

👨‍⚕️ CONNECT TO REAL DOCTORS
Links to free official services (eSanjeevani in India, NHS 111 in the UK, healthdirect in Australia, 811 in Canada), plus nearby heart doctors, hospitals and pharmacies on your maps app. The app also writes questions for your next appointment from your own data.

🩸 BLOOD DONATION
• Donor card showing when you can give again
• Blood-group compatibility chart
• Build an urgent blood request and share it to WhatsApp groups
• Donation log, badges and an "I donated" share

👨‍👩‍👧 FAMILY
Look after your parents' or partner's heart health on one phone, each with their own records.

📋 MEDICAL HISTORY AND DOCTOR REPORT
A timeline of your health from birth, plus a one-page report to show any doctor.

🏥 HEALTH-WORKER MODE
For community health workers and NGOs: screen many people in a row, record measured blood pressure, flag referrals and export the results as a CSV.

🌍 8 LANGUAGES
English, हिन्दी, Español, Français, Português, العربية, Kiswahili, 中文

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Works offline.

Heart Risk Check is a screening and self-care tool. It does not diagnose disease or replace a doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

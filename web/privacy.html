Privacy Policy

Heart Risk Check · Last updated 18 October 2026

In short: Heart Risk Check has no accounts, no servers that store your data, no ads and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your own phone.

What the app stores

The app saves what you enter in your device's browser storage, on your phone only. That includes your profile, blood-pressure readings, medicines, weight, activity, heart checks, medical ID, medical history, blood-donation records, family members' profiles and health-worker screening lists. We do not receive, collect or sell any of it.

Your location

The app reads your location only when you tap Get my location in the SOS section. It puts a map link in a message that you choose to send to your emergency contact by WhatsApp or SMS. The app does not store your location or send it anywhere else.

Services the app links to

Some buttons open other services: your phone app (to call emergency numbers), WhatsApp, SMS, Google Maps and official health services such as eSanjeevani, NHS 111 and healthdirect. Once you open them, their own privacy policies apply. The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google can see your device's IP address when the app first loads.

Backups and deleting your data

You can export a backup file from Profile → Backup. That file stays wherever you save it. To delete everything, remove a person in Profile → Family, clear the app's site data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app.

Health information

Heart Risk Check is a screening and self-care tool. It does not diagnose disease and does not replace a doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Children

The app is meant for adults aged 18 and over. A parent or carer can use it on a child's behalf.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
